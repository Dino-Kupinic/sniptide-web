# Compatible tool logos

The marquee uses artwork from official sites and brand kits, retrieved on
5 November 2026. Lettering is part of the artwork; no brand names are recreated
with the site's font. The canvas applies the same hatched fill to the complete
logo, including its edges. The source artwork also serves as the loading fallback.

SVGs retain their original paths and proportions, with accessible names added to
the root elements. The dither renderer uses the source alpha channel and the page's
foreground color. Codex links to its product page and keeps its accessible name;
the available OpenAI brand kit supplies its artwork rather than a custom Codex
wordmark. Slack's official lockup is supplied as a PNG.

These are compatibility links. The marks belong to their respective owners.
