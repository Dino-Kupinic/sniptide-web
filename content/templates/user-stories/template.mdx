## What each part does

| Part | What it settles | How to write it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| As a ... | Whose problem this is, and which permissions apply. | Use a role that affects the outcome, such as “workspace owner”. |
| I want ... | The capability, in the user's words. | Keep one outcome per story. Split work when the outcomes can be released independently. |
| So that ... | The reason, which settles the small choices the story doesn't cover. | Name the outcome the person cares about. |
| Acceptance criteria | When the story is done. | Given a starting state, when the user acts, then a visible result. |
| Notes | Facts the team would otherwise ask about. | Defaults, limits and names. |
| Out of scope | What the story deliberately leaves out. | The features people will assume come along. |

Use the outcome to decide which checks belong in the story. The invite example gives a teammate their own access, so the criteria check the invited identity and role as well as email delivery.

## Write criteria someone can check

Each criterion should pass or fail with no discussion. "Invites work well" can't fail. "The invite appears under Pending and the person receives an email with a link" can. Cover duplicate invitations, full workspaces and expired links. When a story changes access, try it with a person who lacks permission and verify that the system denies the change.

Exact copy belongs in the criteria when it matters, as in the expired-link message above. A developer or an agent will otherwise write its own.

## Keep stories small

Split a large story by behavior someone can try, such as sending, accepting or revoking an invite. Put rules that span stories, such as seat reservations, in one shared section.

## Variant: a story in an issue

When stories live in your tracker instead of the repository, the same parts fit in an issue body:

```md title="Issue body"
**As a** workspace owner, **I want** to revoke a pending invite, **so that** the wrong person
can't join.

**Done when**
- [ ] A revoked invite leaves Pending and frees its reserved seat.
- [ ] Its link shows “This invite is no longer valid. Ask a workspace owner for a new one.”
```

For a feature larger than a few stories, write a [PRD](/templates/prd-for-agents) and keep the stories inside it.
