## Lead with what changed

Most readers see the title and the first sentence, in a feed, an inbox or a search result. Put the change there: "Owners can now invite their team" beats "We're excited to share a big update". Show the screen if it helps the reader find the feature. Use sample data in the screenshot and write alt text that describes the visible change.

## What each part of the post does

| Part | What it does | How to write it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | Names the change from the reader's side. | A verb and the feature: "Invite your team to Acme". |
| Opening paragraph | Says what was true before and what is true now. | Two or three sentences. A reader who stops here should know what shipped. |
| Screenshot | Shows the feature. | The real screen with realistic data, and alt text that describes it. |
| What you can do | The capabilities, one per line. | Bold the action, then one sentence of detail. |
| Availability | Who gets it and when. | Name eligible plans, regions or rollout stages where they affect access. Include pricing when it changes. |
| Try it | One next step. | Where to click, plus a link to the docs. One call to action, not three. |

The files use a fictional rollout. Confirm the launch date, plan, role permissions and seat limits with the release owner. For a staged rollout, replace “now” with the date or condition when the reader gains access.

## Write each social post for its place

Keep one benefit and one link in the short post. Open the LinkedIn version with the reader's use case. In email, use the preview to add detail the subject leaves out.

Publish the main post first. Open the final URL in a signed-out browser, confirm it works, then use it in each channel. Preview the link card and check the length after replacing the sample copy.

## Variant: a short in-app announcement

For a smaller feature, a banner or a what's-new item is enough:

```md title="whats-new.md"
**Export invoices to CSV.** The export on the Invoices page now keeps your filters.
[See how](https://example.com/docs/export)
```

Follow your [brand voice guide](/templates/brand-voice-guide) for the wording, and add the change to your [changelog](/templates/release-notes) in the same release.
